Fridge Days · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Short version: Fridge Days does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your food list stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Fridge Days has no server, no account system, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see what's in your fridge, and no one else can either.

Where your data lives

  • Your food items, expiration dates, and your zero-waste stats are stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Any data that ever leaves the app does so only because you actively export or share it yourself.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only for the optional day-before and day-of expiration reminders. Processed entirely on your device.
  • No other permissions are requested. The app does not access your photos, contacts, location, or any other data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.

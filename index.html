Fridge Days · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026 · Updated for AI Scan

Short version: everything stays on your device — except a photo, and only if you tap "AI Scan". That photo is sent to an identification service, used once, and never stored.

AI Scan (what leaves your device)

  • When you use the AI Scan feature, the photo you choose is compressed and sent to our processing service (a proxy we control) which forwards it to an AI vision model to identify foods.
  • The photo is used only to generate the food list you see. It is not stored on our servers, not used for training, and not shared with anyone else.
  • What comes back is a short list of food names and estimated nutrition — nothing else.
  • If you never use AI Scan, nothing ever leaves your device.

What we collect

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no identifiers. We cannot see your food list, and no one else can either.

Where your data lives

  • Your food items, expiration dates, calories, and zero-waste stats are stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Photos attached to items are stored locally as thumbnails only.
  • The small photo you send for AI Scan is processed in transit and discarded — we keep no history of scans.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you point it at food for AI Scan. Processed as described above.
  • Photo Library: used only when you pick an existing photo for AI Scan. We access only the photo you select.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional day-before and day-of expiry reminders, processed entirely on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Calorie estimates

Nutrition values shown are good-faith estimates calibrated with USDA reference data. They are informational only and not medical or dietary advice.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.
